# FleetSense 2025 Findings Report (complete after running)

## Executive summary
Add 2–3 evidence-based findings from your dashboard.

## Data and quality
Report source months, input rows, quarantined rows, pass rate and the top quarantine reasons.

## Findings (maximum six charts)
1. Trips by month.
2. Trips by weekday.
3. Trips by pickup hour.
4. Top 10 pickup zones.
5. Bottom 10 pickup zones (state the minimum-trip threshold).
6. Revenue with charges vs base fare by zone.

## Recommendations (maximum three)
Write recommendations only after inspecting the actual results. Do not invent findings.

## Limitations
This dataset describes completed trips, not unserved ride requests or available vehicles. Demand and utilisation are therefore proxies.
